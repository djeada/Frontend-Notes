# How to read the UX figures

These are diagrammatic examples, not captured usability-test evidence. Colors and dimensions are deliberate teaching choices and not compliance claims. For each figure, read its accompanying prose in [`notes/06_ux.md`](../../../notes/06_ux.md), predict how a particular change might affect a task, build a working example, and check the outcome with representative users. Avoid interpreting a law or heuristic as an instruction to remove necessary choices, shrink every sidebar, or force an exact color ratio.
